## Intent

A 36-second sample for CyberKnight's training video *Module 1: Product Management Fundamentals &
Soft Skills*, doubling as a motion-design showreel. Realistic, cinematic, not toy-like: the only
character on screen is CyberKnight's armoured knight mascot (black/gunmetal armour, red neon
seams, eared helmet, glowing red visor) in a dark, rain-lashed high-tech tower. He also narrates
(deep male voice). Every teaching beat is a red hologram or kinetic type over the footage.
